When you search for a villa in St. Vincent and the Grenadines, the first results you see are usually from Booking.com, Airbnb, or Expedia. It feels convenient — one search, dozens of options, easy comparison. But that convenience comes at a cost, and most of the time, that cost is yours.

Here is why booking directly with Stonetrail Villas is always the smarter choice.

## You Pay Less When You Book Direct

Every booking platform charges a commission — typically between 15% and 25% of the room rate. That commission does not disappear. It is either absorbed into the price you see, added as a service fee at checkout, or both.

When you book directly at [stonetrailvillas.com](https://stonetrailvillas.com), there is no middleman taking a cut. Our best available rate is always on our own site, and we guarantee it. If you find a lower rate elsewhere for the same dates and villa, contact us — we will match it.

## You Get Personal Service from the Start

Booking through a platform means your enquiry goes into a queue. You might get an automated response. You might wait 24 hours. You might deal with a support team that has never visited St. Vincent.

When you book direct, you communicate with our team directly — the same people who will welcome you on arrival, who know every villa, every view, and every detail of the property. Have a question about the best villa for a family of five? Want to know which suite has the best sunset view? We can answer that immediately, because we live it.

## Your Special Requests Actually Get Heard

Platforms have standardised booking forms. There is a notes field, but there is no guarantee anyone reads it before your arrival.

Direct bookings are different. When you contact us directly, we can discuss early check-in, late check-out, special dietary requirements for a welcome basket, or arranging a private transfer from the airport. These are the details that turn a good holiday into an exceptional one — and they are only possible when you book direct.

## No Hidden Fees at Checkout

One of the most frustrating parts of booking through a platform is the checkout page. The price you saw in the search results rarely matches the total after service fees, cleaning fees, and platform charges are added.

With Stonetrail Villas, the price we quote is the price you pay. No surprises.

## A Complimentary Welcome Awaits

As a thank-you for booking direct, every direct guest receives a complimentary welcome amenity on arrival. It is a small gesture, but it reflects how we feel about guests who choose to support us directly rather than through a third party.

## Priority Access to the Best Villas

Our most sought-after villas — the Presidential Suite, the Honeymoon Suite, the Superior Villa — are released to direct enquiries first. By the time availability appears on a booking platform, the best dates are often already gone.

Booking direct means you get first access.
